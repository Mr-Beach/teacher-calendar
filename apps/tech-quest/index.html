Tech
Quest

Seven stops. Do them in order. Check each one off as you finish.

0/7

Clever

You only do these once. After setup, you'll open Savvas and MagicSchool from Schoology.

  1. 1

    Heart your apps

    Open Clever. Find Savvas Realize and MagicSchool. Click the heart on each one.

    Check: both apps now show up in your favorites.
  2. 2

    Set up Savvas Realize

    In Clever, click Savvas Realize. Sign in and finish any setup screens.

    Check: you can see the Savvas home page.
  3. 3

    Ask MagicSchool

    Go back to Clever. Click MagicSchool. Ask it a math question, like something from class you want explained another way.

    Check: read its answer. Did it help?

Schoology

This is where you'll find everything for math, every day.

  1. 4

    Open the calendar

    Math course → calendar link at the top of the page

    Find: what is the next lesson?
  2. 5

    Explore a topic folder

    Math course → a Topic folder (try the one we're on now)

    Open each folder below and look for:

    • Launch: the Math Walk video that starts the topic, and a readiness assessment
    • Review: review problems for the topic
    • A lesson folder: the Key Concept PDF and Practice and Problem Solving (sometimes homework, sometimes extra)
    Note: what else did you find in Launch and Review? Bonus: peek inside the Topic 1 folder.
  3. 6

    Find your textbook

    Math course → Whole Year Materials

    Open the Interactive Student Edition: Grade 6. That's your math book, online.

    Check: flip to any page.
  4. 7

    Play a game

    Whole Year Materials → Savvas games link

    Try the Topic 2 game. You earned it.

Finish line

Check off all seven stops to cross it.
